Private

Your stuff is nobody's business.

No accounts, no tracking, no ads. The policy is short because the app simply does not collect much.

On your device, in your iCloud.

Tiles, photos, and videos live on your iPhone and sync to your own private iCloud under your Apple ID. Nothing is stored on our servers — we cannot read, access, or recover your data.

Everything runs on the phone.

Scanning, text recognition, and Ask work on-device — your questions and your inventory are never sent to us or to any third-party service.

Your media stays yours.

Photos and videos you add never leave your device and your iCloud — and Backup builds one zip whenever you want a copy out.
