Send feedback from TestFlight

While QR Tiles is in beta, feedback goes straight to the developer through TestFlight:

  • Take a screenshot in QR Tiles, tap the preview, and choose Share Beta Feedback.
  • Or open TestFlight, choose QR Tiles, and tap Send Beta Feedback.

If the app crashes, TestFlight offers to send the crash report with a note. Those reports are read.

Before you write

  • Help has the same answers the app shows behind its ⓘ marks.
  • Your tiles live on your iPhone and in your own iCloud, so nobody here can see or recover them. A backup from Settings is the copy you control. See Privacy.
