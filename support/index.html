Support

Questions, problems and ideas

Write from inside the app

Support is two taps away in QR Tiles: open Settings, then Support. It opens an email to the developer with your app version already in the subject, so the answer fits the build you have. Every message is read and answered by the person who made the app.

If the app crashes and your iPhone shares analytics with app developers (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), the crash report reaches us, and those reports are read.

Without the app at hand, write to info@newstuff.app.

Before you write

  • Help has the same answers the app shows behind its ⓘ marks.
  • Your tiles live on your iPhone and in your own iCloud, so nobody here can see or recover them. A backup from Settings is the copy you control. See Privacy.
