Get help with QR Tiles

Need help? Start with the Help guide — it covers everything from your first tile to printing, scanning, and backup.

Found a bug or have a request?

While QR Tiles is in TestFlight, send feedback straight from TestFlight — open the TestFlight app, pick QR Tiles, and tap Send Beta Feedback (screenshots come along automatically). Include:

  • What you did, what you expected, what happened
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • The app version (shown at the bottom of the Home screen)
  • A screenshot if it's visual

We're a small (very small) shop; you'll hear back from a human.

Subscription issues

Billing, refunds, and restores are handled by Apple: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions, or reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy questions

See the Privacy policy — the short version is that your data stays on your device and in your own iCloud, and we couldn't peek if we wanted to.
