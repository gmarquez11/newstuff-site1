Get started

QR Tiles keeps track of what's inside your drawers, boxes and bins. You stick a QR code on each container. Scanning it opens a page for that container — a tile — with photos of what's inside, a title and labels you can search.

What you need

  • An iPhone with iOS 27 or later, and QR Tiles from the App Store.
  • A printer and US Letter label sheets: R001 (160 small labels per sheet) or 5160-size address labels (30 per sheet, such as Avery 5160). Plain paper is fine for a test page.
  • To use more than one device, or to share, sign in to iCloud.

Words used in the app

Tile
One container and what you know about it: photos and videos, a title, labels and where it lives.
Label
A word or phrase you add to a tile, such as “batteries” or “winter gloves”. Search and Ask look through labels. (The sticker on the box is the QR code.)
Group and zone
Where a tile lives. A group is a shelf, cabinet or corner; a zone is the room or floor that holds groups.
Site
One place you keep track of, such as your home, a workshop or a storage unit. Each site has its own tiles, codes and lists, and a site is what you share with other people. Some screens in the app call it your home.
Batch
One printing of new QR codes.

Set up your first tile

  1. On Home, tap QR Code Batches.
  2. Under Label stock, choose R001 or 5160 to match your label sheets.
  3. Tap + (New batch), then Create New Batch. The sheet of new codes opens for printing.
  4. Print it and stick a code on a container.
  5. Tap the Scan tab and point the camera at that code.
  6. The new tile opens with its title ready for typing. Type a title, such as “Garage shelf 2 — paint”.
  7. Tap Take photo to photograph what's inside, or Add photo or video to choose from your library.
  8. Tap + in the Labels card, type a few words for what's inside (one per line) and tap Add.
  9. To say where it lives, tap No group under the title and choose a group, or New Group….

Result The title is saved when you leave the field, and labels, photos and the group are saved as you add them — there's no Save button. Scanning the code again opens this tile, and search and Ask can find it by its title, labels and photos.

Tip Before using real label sheets, print a test page on plain paper and check that it lines up. See Line up your labels.

Four tabs run along the bottom of the screen:

The camera, for scanning codes and taking photos. See Scan a code.

Your site, backups, appearance, scanning, Ask, plans and support. See Settings at a glance.

Wherever you see ⓘ in the app, tap it for a short note about that part of the screen.

Accessibility

  • QR Tiles follows your iPhone's text size. At the largest sizes, rows stack so nothing is cut off.
  • With VoiceOver, the commands in touch-and-hold menus and swipe actions — on photos, labels, list items and codes — are available as actions on the item.
  • Ask can read its answers aloud. For a more natural voice, download one in the iPhone Settings app under Accessibility → Read & Speak → Voices.

Scan a code

The Scan tab is the main way to scan. A tile's own page also has a Scan button.

  1. Tap Scan.
  2. Point the camera at a QR code and hold steady until it reads.
  3. If a label is damaged or out of reach, type its six-character code in Or type a code (e.g. SP1CE6) and tap Go.

What a scan does

A blank code from this site
Becomes a new tile, which opens with its title ready for typing.
A code that's on a tile
Opens the tile. Scan Redirect decides where you land on its page.
A tile in Recently Deleted
Comes back and opens: This tile was in Recently Deleted. Opening it restored it.
A code from another of your sites
QR Tiles tells you it belongs elsewhere (That QR code belongs to another home) and offers to switch to that site.
A code in more than one of your sites
This usually follows a restore. That code is in more than one home lets you choose which one to open.
A retired code
That label was retired — it no longer stands for anything. See Reprint, retire or move a code.
A QR Tiles code none of your sites printed
QR Tiles asks before adding it. See Codes QR Tiles didn't print.
A custom code you attached to a tile
Opens that tile. See Use a QR code that's already there.
Any other QR code
Nothing readable in that code. To use it, attach it to a tile first.

Good to know

  • QR Tiles reads QR codes, not bar codes.
  • Scanning needs the camera. If you see Camera needed, allow the camera for QR Tiles in the iPhone Settings app.
  • QR Tiles codes have six letters and numbers and never use the letters I, L, O or U. When you type a code, I and L are read as 1, and O as 0.
  • Only QR Tiles codes can be typed. Custom codes have to be scanned.
  • If you see Couldn't check that code just now — try the scan again., wait a moment and scan again.

Scan settings

Open Settings → Scanning, or tap Scan settings at the top right of the scanner. These choices apply on this iPhone.

Scan Redirect
What happens when you scan a tile that's already set up. The tile always opens; choose Just open the tile, Focus the title to start typing, or Open add labels.
Needs Picture
What happens when a blank code becomes a new tile. Normally its title is ready for typing. Turn on Straight into the camera to take its first photo instead.
Unlisted Code
What happens with QR Tiles codes none of your sites printed. Normally QR Tiles asks first; turn on Add unrecognized codes automatically to skip the question. Adopt into chooses where those codes go: Misc (automatic) or one of your batches.

Photo first, then scan

Sometimes it's easier to photograph a drawer before you find its label. The scanner can hold one photo and add it to the next tile you scan.

  1. On the Scan tab, switch to CAPTURE and take the photo.
  2. The scanner shows Photo attaches to the next scan.
  3. Scan the container's code. The photo is added to that tile, and you see Photo added to “…”.
  • Changed your mind? Tap Drop the photo.
  • If you leave the scanner while a photo is waiting, QR Tiles asks Discard the waiting photo? Choose Discard or Keep scanning.
  • If the next scan can't take the photo — a retired code, for example — the photo is let go and the message says so.

Fill in a tile

A tile's page shows its photos and videos at the top, then its title, its group and its labels. In a shared site where you can only view, these controls are turned off.

Title
Tap it to type; it's saved when you leave the field. If you see ✱ another tile has this title, that's allowed, but different titles make search and Ask answers clearer.
Group
Tap the line under the title (No group on a new tile) and choose a group, None or New Group…. A group made here has no zone yet; you can give it one in Organize.
More (•••)
Includes Show Media Tags, Print Label, Attach Custom QR, Add to List and Delete Tile. A deleted tile waits in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

Labels

  • Tap + in the Labels card, type one label per line and tap Add.
  • Tap a label to change its text. Swipe left on a label to delete it.
  • After you change or delete a label, Undo appears for a few seconds.

Draft labels

Labels suggested by an outside tool (see Retag photos with an outside tool) arrive as drafts. They don't count as your labels until you accept them: use Accept label to keep one or Delete label to drop it. To find every tile with drafts, use the Draft labels filter under Needs attention.

If a change can't be saved, QR Tiles tells you: Couldn't save that change — try again.

Photos and videos

  • Take photo opens the camera. Add photo or video (Add more once the tile has some) opens your photo library.
  • Touch and hold a photo or video for Move Left, Move Right, Edit Media Tags and Delete Photo or Delete Video. Photos also have Rotate and Crop….
  • After cropping, tap Use. The cropped photo is kept and the original goes to Recently Deleted.
  • Deleted photos and videos wait in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

Media tags

On your iPhone, QR Tiles tags each photo with what it sees and reads any printed words in it. Search and Ask use these tags and words even when they're hidden.

  • To see them under each photo and in List rows, turn on Show media tags in Settings → Tiles display, or tap More (•••) → Show Media Tags on a tile.
  • To fix them, choose Edit Media Tags and edit Tags, one per line and Text in the photo.
  • If photo matches make Ask guess too much, turn off Match photo tags & text in Ask settings.

Good to know Photos are stored at up to 2048 pixels on the long side and videos at up to 1080p. Location data is removed from photos you pick from your library.

Search and filter

The Search tab lists every tile in the site you're in. Choose how to see them: Photo, List, Labels (each label with the tile it's on) or Ask.

Search

  • Type in Search tiles, labels, codes…. QR Tiles looks in titles, labels, codes, group and zone names, and the tags and words found in photos.
  • What you type is matched as one piece of text: “gloves” finds “winter gloves”, but “wool gloves” finds only tiles with that exact phrase. Capital letters don't matter; accents do.
  • To ask a question instead, see Ask where something is.

Filters

Tap Filters and check what you want to see. Clear turns them all off.

Sort
Zone · Group · Title, Title A–Z or Newest first.
Recent
Tiles scanned or added in the last 30 days, newest first. While it's on, it sets the order.
Needs attention
Without photos, Untitled and Draft labels.
Media
With video.
Zones and Groups
Check one or more.

Within Zones or within Groups, a tile can match any one you check. Different kinds of filters combine: a zone plus Without photos shows only tiles in that zone that have no photos. Search works inside the filters; Ask always looks at the whole site.

To shorten the panel, open Settings → Tiles display. Turning off a section there hides it from the panel but doesn't change what's being filtered. Hide rows with zero tiles leaves out empty rows, and Show labels and Show media tags choose what List rows show.

Ask where something is

Ask answers questions like “Where are the pots?” by naming the places most likely to hold what you're looking for.

  1. On Home, tap Ask, or choose Ask on the Search tab.
  2. Type your question in the search bar. “pots” works as well as “where are the pots”.
  3. Read the answer, and tap a place under Places to open to open that tile.
  • Read aloud speaks the answer; Share answer sends it.
  • A place marked From a photo was found only in what QR Tiles read in its photos, so check it before you rely on it.
  • Ask searches the whole site you're in, whatever filters are set.
  • Short questions that name the thing work best.

Ask settings

In Settings → Ask:

Places per answer
Top match, Up to 3 (the starting choice) or Up to 5.
Answer as a list
One line per place instead of a sentence.
Match photo tags & text
On at first. Turn it off if photo matches bring in too many guesses.
Answer with
Apple Intelligence picks the best of the matching places, on your iPhone. Off answers by matching words. If Apple Intelligence is dimmed, the note below it says why — for example, this iPhone doesn't support it.

Either way, your questions and tiles stay on your iPhone. You can also ask from Siri, Control Center and Apple Watch.

Work with several tiles

  1. On the Search tab, narrow things down with search or filters if you like, then tap Select.
  2. Tap tiles to select them, or use Select all.
  3. Choose an action from the buttons at the bottom:
    • Print prints a tile label for each one, using your last Print Label choices.
    • Move puts them all in one group. Zones are reached through groups, so you pick a group.
    • Add to List adds them to a list you pick; tap Save to List.
    • Delete asks first, then moves them to Recently Deleted. Undo appears for a few seconds.
  4. Tap Done when you're finished.

Lists

A list gathers tiles for one job, such as packing for a trip or tidying the garage, and lets you tick them off as you go. Creating and editing lists is part of Plus; on Basic you can still read them.

  1. On Home, tap Lists, then New list, and give it a name.
  2. Tap Add Tiles and pick the tiles. You can also add from a tile (More → Add to List) or from a selection.
  3. As you go, touch and hold an item and choose Close. Choose Reopen to undo it.
  • Swipe right on an item to add a Note, or left to Remove it.
  • Remove takes the tile off the list; Close keeps it on the list as done. Neither changes the tile itself.
  • Show closed items shows what you've finished. Items are counted under Open and Closed.
  • Add a note about this list holds a note for the whole list.
  • Delete List deletes the list and its progress. The tiles stay where they are.
  • Lists belong to a site, so the people you share it with see them. To edit lists, each person needs Plus on their own Apple Account; otherwise they see You can read these lists.

Print QR code sheets

Before you start

  • US Letter label sheets: R001 (160 labels, 1 × ½ inch, 8 across and 20 down) or 5160 (30 address labels, 2⅝ × 1 inch, such as Avery 5160).
  • A printer your iPhone can print to.
  1. On Home, tap QR Code Batches.
  2. Under Label stock, choose R001 or 5160. The line below shows how many labels are on a sheet.
  3. Tap + (New batch). QR Tiles tells you how many blank codes you already have and asks Create a new batch?
  4. Tap Create New Batch. The new codes are saved and the sheet opens for printing.
  5. Print it, then stick a code on each container. Codes stay blank until you scan them.

Result The batch appears under Batches, where you can see which of its codes are claimed, blank or retired.

Good to know

  • A new batch always has new codes — as many as one sheet of the chosen stock holds. To print the same codes again, open the batch and tap Sheet PDF instead of making another batch.
  • Sheet PDF uses the label stock chosen now, and adds pages if the batch has more codes than fit on one sheet.
  • If the sheet didn't print, the batch and its codes are still saved. Open it under Batches and tap Sheet PDF.
  • If your printer's options include scaling, print at 100% so the codes line up with the labels.

Line up your labels

  1. In QR Code Batches, choose your Label stock.
  2. Tap More (•••) → Print a test page and print it on plain paper.
  3. Hold the page against a window with a label sheet behind it.
  4. If the codes sit too high or too low, adjust Row 1 top under Calibration. If they drift as you go down the page, adjust Row pitch. Print another test page to check.

Reset calibration to defaults, in the same More menu, puts both back. Each label stock keeps its own calibration on this iPhone.

Print a label for one tile

Besides the small QR codes, you can print a bigger label for a tile, with its photos, title and QR code — handy for boxes and bins.

  1. Open the tile and tap More (•••) → Print Label.
  2. Choose Per Page (1, 2 or 4), how many Photos (0 to 4) and where Photos sit, and whether to show the Title, QR code, Zone, Group, Date, Labels and Blank lines to write on.
  3. The preview updates as you go. Print, then cut the copies apart.

Your choices are kept for the next label, and for printing labels for several tiles at once.

Reprint, retire or move a code

Each tile has one QR Tiles code, and every code belongs to the batch it was printed in. To manage a code, open Home → QR Code Batches and tap its batch. Codes are listed under Claimed (on a tile — tap one to open the tile), Blank and Retired. Touch and hold a code for:

Reprint
Prints that same code again, for a damaged label. Nothing about the tile changes.
Retire
Takes a code out of service, for a label that's lost or on the wrong box. Scanning it then says That label was retired — it no longer stands for anything. The tile and everything on it stay.
Put Back
Returns a retired code to service.
Move
Moves a claimed code to a tile that has no code: Move This QR Code lists the Tiles with no QR code yet. The tile it lands on keeps it from then on.
  • A tile holds one QR Tiles code. To move a code onto a tile that already has one, retire that tile's code first.
  • A retired code can't move; put it back first.
  • Custom QR codes can't be reprinted or moved this way. See Use a QR code that's already there.

Use a QR code that's already there

A box may already carry a QR code — from a store, a moving company or another app. Attach it to a tile, and scanning it opens that tile.

  1. Open the tile and tap More (•••) → Attach Custom QR.
  2. Scan the code with your iPhone's camera.

Result From then on, scanning that code on the Scan tab opens this tile. The tile keeps its own QR Tiles code too.

  • If the code is already on another tile, QR Tiles names that tile and asks before moving it here.
  • QR Tiles codes (six characters) can't be attached this way. Scan those on the Scan tab.
  • Use codes that belong to one box only. A code printed on every copy of a product would open the same tile for every copy.
  • Custom codes are included in backups. Restoring can move one to the restored tile; see Restore from a backup.

Codes QR Tiles didn't print

You may scan a QR Tiles code that none of your sites printed — for example, labels printed on another iPhone, or a sheet someone gave you.

  1. Scan it. QR Tiles says That code isn't recognized.
  2. Tap Add to “…” to add it to the site you're in.

Result The code becomes a new tile, just like a blank code from your own sheet.

  • Added codes go into an automatic Misc batch (up to 160 codes each), or into the batch chosen under Adopt into in Scan settings.
  • To add them without being asked, turn on Add unrecognized codes automatically.
  • A code from another of your sites isn't added here; QR Tiles offers to switch to that site instead.
  • If you see Couldn't add that code, try again.

Zones and groups

A group is where a tile sits — a shelf, cabinet or corner. A zone is a room or floor that holds groups. Tiles belong to groups, and a tile's zone is its group's zone.

  1. On Home, tap Organize.
  2. Tap +, choose New zone or New group, and type a name.
  3. Next to each group, tap its zone (No zone at first) to choose one.
  • Tap a name to rename it.
  • Swipe left to delete. Deleting a group leaves its tiles without a group; deleting a zone keeps its groups. Undo appears for a few seconds.
  • To put one tile in a group, use the line under its title. For many tiles, select them and tap Move.

Sites

A site is one place you keep track of. Each site has its own tiles, codes, groups, zones, lists and accent color, and you share a site as a whole. Some screens in the app call a site your home.

  • Switch sites: tap the site's name at the top left of Home, then tap another site. Search, Ask and Siri work with the site you're in.
  • Add a site: in the same menu, tap + (New Site) and name it. The new site starts empty; your other sites don't change.
  • Site Manager: tap the gear in the Sites menu, or go to Settings → Site Manager. Here you rename the site, see what it holds, manage sharing, check iCloud sync, and open Logs and Store Check and External Tools.
  • Delete a site: Site Manager → Delete Site. QR Tiles first asks you to save a backup (Back Up First). Deleting is permanent on every device the site syncs to, and the backup is the only way back. You can't delete your only site.
  • In a shared site, only the owner can rename or delete it. Members can Leave Site instead.

Share a site

Sharing invites people into a whole site: every tile, photo, video, label and list, including everything added later. You can't share only part of a site.

Before you start

  • You need Plus. Only the owner subscribes; the people you invite don't pay to join. See Plans.
  • You and each person you invite need iCloud.
  • Look through the site's photos. Drawer photos can show documents, medicines or valuables.
  • Photos and videos that members add use the owner's iCloud storage.
  1. Open Settings → Site Manager.
  2. Under Sharing, tap Invite with AirDrop, Messages or Mail….
  3. Read what sharing means, turn on I understand what sharing means, and tap Invite with AirDrop, Messages or Mail… again.
  4. The first time, QR Tiles asks you to Back Up First. Tap Save Backup Now and keep the zip somewhere you control, such as Files or iCloud Drive. Nothing is shared until the backup is saved.
  5. QR Tiles then prepares the site for sharing. This happens once and can take a few minutes for a big site, so keep the app open.
  6. Choose how to send the invitation and who gets it. In the sharing options you can let people make changes or only view.

Result When someone accepts, they appear under Sharing in Site Manager, and the site appears on their iPhone.

Join a shared site

  1. Install QR Tiles and make sure you're signed in to iCloud.
  2. Open the invitation and tap its link. QR Tiles opens and accepts it.
  3. Keep the app open while the site downloads. Until it arrives, Home shows Your household is still arriving. Photos can keep arriving after the tiles.
  • You don't need Plus to join, or to make changes if the owner allows it. Lists are the exception: editing them needs Plus on your own Apple Account.
  • If the owner made you view-only, you can look but not change anything.
  • QR Tiles can notify you when someone else changes something. It asks for permission once; you can turn this off with Tell me when someone else changes something in Site Manager.
  • To leave, open Site Manager and tap Leave Site. The site goes away from your devices; to come back, you'll need a new invitation.

Manage members

In Settings → Site Manager, Sharing lists everyone with their status: Owner, Invited, Accepted or Removed.

  • Invite or manage members… invites more people or changes what they can do.
  • Revoke, next to a person, removes them from the site. The site leaves their devices, but anything they already saw or copied stays with them.
  • The Timeline shows who changed what. Each person's name comes from Your name in their Settings.

If Plus ends

Sharing runs on the owner's Plus subscription. If it ends:

  1. For the first 7 days, sharing keeps working.
  2. After that, sharing stops. The owner's QR Tiles removes the members, and the site leaves their devices. Members see No longer shared.
  3. Until every member is removed, the owner sees This site is shared, and Plus has ended. with two choices: Start Plus again, or Remove all shares and keep using the site for free.

Result Nothing in the site is deleted. Every tile, photo and label stays with the owner.

Starting Plus again doesn't bring members back on its own. Everyone you invited will need to accept a new invitation, which you send from Site Manager → Sharing.

Timeline

The Timeline shows what happened in the site you're in and, on a shared site, who did it. Open it with the bell on Home or from the Sites menu.

  • The chips at the top choose what to show: Create, Update, Delete and View. View covers activity such as scans, searches and Ask questions, and stays hidden until you turn it on.
  • Tap an entry for its details.
  • Changes are kept for as long as the site exists. Activity is kept for 90 days.
  • Most changes are recorded: tiles, labels, photos and videos, codes, groups, zones, lists and batches. Some small edits, such as rotating a photo or changing an order, may not appear.

Recently Deleted and Undo

Deleted tiles, photos and videos wait in Recently Deleted for 30 days, then are removed for good.

  1. Open Settings → Recently Deleted, or tap the trash can in the Sites menu.
  2. Find the tile, photo or video and tap Restore.
  • Scanning a deleted tile's code also brings it back.
  • Items in Recently Deleted still use storage until they're removed.
  • Labels, groups and zones don't go to Recently Deleted. When you delete one — or delete tiles from a selection — Undo appears for a few seconds. After that, label, group and zone deletions are final.
  • Deleting a list asks first and can't be undone.

Back up a site

A backup is one zip file with every photo and video in the site, plus all its data as CSV files. It's the copy you control: keep it in Files, iCloud Drive or anywhere else.

  1. Make sure you're in the site you want to back up. A backup covers one site.
  2. Open Settings and tap Back Up Now (it's in the Sites menu too).
  3. If some photos haven't finished downloading, QR Tiles waits for them: Waiting for photos to finish syncing…
  4. When the share sheet appears, save the zip — for example, with Save to Files.
  • If photos are still missing after the wait, the backup stops rather than save an incomplete file. Leave QR Tiles open on Wi-Fi for a while and try again.
  • A backup holds everything in the site, including photos of what's in your drawers. Store and send it with care.
  • QR Tiles also asks for a backup before you share a site for the first time and before you delete one.

Back to contents

Restore from a backup

Restoring builds a new, separate site from a backup zip. It doesn't replace or merge into a site you already have.

  1. Open Settings → Restore from Backup, or use restore in the Sites menu.
  2. Tap Choose Backup Zip and pick the file.
  3. Wait while QR Tiles shows Restoring…. When it's done, it shows what came back.
  4. Tap Switch to It to open the new site, or Stay Here.

Before you restore

  • Restoring the same backup twice makes two sites. You can delete the one you don't need.
  • If the original site is still on this iPhone, both sites have the same QR Tiles codes. Scanning one of them asks which site to open (That code is in more than one home).
  • Custom QR codes go with the restored tiles. If one is still attached to a tile in another site on this iPhone, it moves to the restored tile, so scanning it opens the restored copy.
  • If you see Restore failed, your other sites are left as they were.

Retag photos with an outside tool

If you want better photo tags than QR Tiles finds on its own, you can export a site's photos to a tool you choose — an AI assistant, a service or a person — and import the tags it writes. Look for it in Settings → Site Manager → External Tools.

Before you start

  • The photos leave QR Tiles. Whoever gets the file can see them, so treat it like a backup.
  • Only photos are exported, not videos.
  1. In External Tools, choose which photos to include and whether to ask for label suggestions too.
  2. Tap Export and give the file to your tool. It includes the photos, instructions for the tool, and a template for its answers.
  3. When the tool returns its file, tap Import, choose the file and confirm with Replace Media Tags.
  4. Review any suggested labels; they arrive as draft labels.
  • Importing replaces the tags and photo text on those photos, including what QR Tiles found on its own. Titles, labels, groups and the photos themselves don't change.
  • This isn't a backup, and it can't move a site.

Siri, Shortcuts and Spotlight

QR Tiles adds these actions to Siri and the Shortcuts app. They work with the site you're in.

Scan a Tile
Opens the scanner. Say “Scan QR Tiles” or “QR Tiles scanner”.
Ask
Asks where something is. Say “Ask QR Tiles” or “Find something in QR Tiles”.
Where is a tile
Tells you where a tile is: “Where is my camping gear in QR Tiles?” If the tile has no group yet, Siri says so.
Search Tiles
Opens the tiles that match. Say “Search QR Tiles” or “Search my tiles in QR Tiles”.
Open Tile
Opens a tile by name: “Open camping gear in QR Tiles”.

Tiles from the site you're in also appear in Spotlight search on your iPhone.

Controls and widgets

Add these the usual way: edit Control Center, the Lock Screen or the Home Screen, and look for QR Tiles.

Scan control
Scan a QR code from Control Center or the Lock Screen, even without unlocking. After a Lock Screen scan you'll see Scanned. Unlock to continue.
Ask control

Plans

Basic (free)
Unlimited tiles, QR codes and printing, plus scanning, search, Ask, the Timeline, backups and restore. You can join sites others share with you, and read lists.
Plus (monthly or annual)
Adds sharing your sites, and creating and editing lists.
  • Only the owner pays for sharing; invited people join free. To create or edit lists, though, each person needs Plus on their own Apple Account.
  • Prices are shown in the app and the App Store before you buy.
  • Manage or cancel in Settings → Upgrades → Manage Subscription. Restore Purchases brings back a subscription after reinstalling or moving to a new iPhone.
  • If Plus ends, nothing is deleted and lists stay readable. For shared sites, see If Plus ends.

  • Where your data lives. Tiles, photos and labels are stored on your iPhone and in your private iCloud. We don't run a server that holds your inventory.

Logs and Store Check

These tools in Site Manager help when something seems off, or when we ask for details.

A record of what the app did. It's off until you turn on Logging, and starts the next time QR Tiles opens. Copy all copies it so you can paste it into an email. Logs can include the names of your tiles and labels, so read before you send.

Troubleshooting

The camera doesn't start, or I see “Camera needed”
Allow the camera for QR Tiles in the iPhone Settings app, then go back to Scan.
A code won't scan
Add light, hold the iPhone steady and a little farther away, and flatten the label. If it's damaged, type its six-character code under Or type a code, then reprint it.
“That code isn't recognized”
None of your sites printed this code. You can add it — see Codes QR Tiles didn't print. If it should be yours, check you're in the right site and that iCloud has finished syncing.
“That QR code belongs to another home” or “That code is in more than one home”
The code is on a tile in another of your sites, or in two sites after a restore. Switch to the site you mean; see Restore from a backup.
“Nothing readable in that code.”
It's not a QR Tiles code and isn't attached to a tile. To use it, attach it to a tile.
Printed codes don't line up with the labels
Check the Label stock, print at 100%, and calibrate with a test page.
Photos are missing on another device
Photos sync through iCloud and can arrive after the tiles. Open QR Tiles on Wi-Fi and give it time. Check iCloud sync in Site Manager, and run Store Check.
The backup didn't finish
Some photos were still downloading. Leave QR Tiles open on Wi-Fi for a few minutes and try again.
An invitation won't open
Make sure QR Tiles is installed and you're signed in to iCloud, then tap the link again. If it still fails, ask the owner for a new invitation.
A shared site disappeared
If you saw No longer shared, the owner removed you, stopped sharing, or their Plus ended. Nothing of yours was deleted; ask the owner for a new invitation.
I can't edit something in a shared site
The owner may have made you view-only. For lists, you need Plus on your own Apple Account.
Apple Intelligence is dimmed in Ask settings
This iPhone doesn't support it, or it isn't turned on. The note under the setting says which. Ask still works with Off.
I deleted something by mistake
Tap Undo if it's still showing, or look in Recently Deleted.

Still stuck?

In the app, go to Settings → Support to start an email with your app version already in the subject, or write to info@newstuff.app. It helps to include:

  • What you were doing, what you tapped and what you saw — the exact message, if there was one.
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • Whether the site is shared, and whether you're its owner.
  • If we ask for a log: turn on Logging in Site Manager → Logs, reopen QR Tiles, repeat the problem, then use Copy all and paste it into your email.

We can't see or recover your inventory — it lives on your iPhone and in your iCloud. Don't send a backup unless you choose to; it contains all your photos. More on the Support page.
