For iPhone

QR Tiles is coming to the App Store. Until then it is in beta on TestFlight.

Joining the beta

If you have a TestFlight invitation, open it on your iPhone and tap Accept, then Install. TestFlight keeps the app up to date as new builds arrive.

What you need

  • An iPhone running iOS 27 or later.
  • iCloud, if you want your tiles on more than one device or want to share a home.
  • For Apple Intelligence answers in Ask, an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence. Ask works on every iPhone without it.

Plans

Basic is free, with unlimited tiles, QR codes and printing. Plus adds household sharing and list editing. The owner subscribes; the people you invite never pay.
