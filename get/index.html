What you need

  • An iPhone running iOS 27 or later.
  • iCloud, if you want your tiles on more than one device or want to share a home.
  • For Apple Intelligence answers in Ask, an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence. Ask works on every iPhone without it.
  • Optional: an Apple Watch with watchOS 26 or later, paired with your iPhone, to ask where things are from your wrist.
  • To print QR codes: a printer and US Letter label sheets, either R001 (160 labels per sheet) or 5160-size address labels (30 per sheet).

Plans

Basic is free, with unlimited tiles, QR codes and printing. Plus adds household sharing and creating and editing lists. The owner subscribes to share, and the people you invite join free. Anyone can read lists; editing them needs Plus on your own Apple Account.
